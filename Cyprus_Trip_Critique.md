# Cyprus Trip — Critique & Recommendations

Actionable items only. Does not change [Cyprus_Trip_Overview.md](Cyprus_Trip_Overview.md).

---

### 1. Wildfire risk — Troodos/Platres/Omodos (Day 6–7)
July 2025 had a fatal wildfire in this exact district (Omodos/Pano Kyvides, 2 deaths). No active fire now, but Cyprus is visibly bracing for a repeat this summer (new Troodos fire-recovery program launched June 2026; EU pre-positioning 777 firefighters across Cyprus for summer 2026).

**Action:** 3–5 days before departure, check [Cyprus Mail's forest-fire coverage](https://cyprus-mail.com/tag/forest-fire/) or the Department of Forests advisory. Have the fallback ready: skip Omodos/Platres, extend Paphos, go straight to Ayia Napa.

### 2. Blue Lagoon boat trip — book the AM slot specifically
Operators (Cpt Marko, Ocean Queen, Ayia Trias Cruises/protarasboattrip.com, +357 99800938) run a real **2-hour morning departure (~9:30am, boarding 8:45–9:15)** to the Blue Lagoon + Konnos Bay.

**Action:** Book that specific AM 2-hour slot, not a 4–5hr full-day version. Returns you to shore by ~11:30–12:30, clearing the entire afternoon before the 17:00 Shabbat-prep deadline — removes the timing risk entirely. Book with free cancellation.

### 3. WaterWorld (Ayia Napa) — assume it's busy in August
Group members flagged uncertainty about slide queues, and there's active resale of pre-bought tickets in the group — a sign it sells out/gets crowded in peak weeks.

**Action:** Buy tickets a few days ahead (already planned), and treat park opening time — not the planned 10:00 — as the actual arrival target, especially for the 4-year-old's patience with queues.

### 4. Day 1 — don't plan a supermarket run the same night
Under the corrected flight time, you land at Elysia Park ~20:05. Squeezing in a grocery run + full dinner for a jet-lagged 4-year-old that same evening is optimistic, and the supermarket closes at 21:00.

**Action:** Skip the Day 1 grocery run — order in or use whatever's in the apartment, and do groceries Day 2 morning instead.

### 5. Day 7 (Troodos) — know what to cut if the day runs long
The original booklet itself flagged this day (Caledonia Falls hike + Troodos Square + Mount Olympus) as possibly too packed for a 4-year-old.

**Action:** If the hike runs long or the 4-year-old is done, cut Mount Olympus first — it's a viewpoint stop, not essential, and the easiest thing to drop without losing the day.
